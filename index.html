How we work

Diagnostic. First hire. Then the crew grows.

  1. 01

    On-site diagnostic

    Kelly walks the process the way a process engineer does: where calls die, where quotes stall, what the techs actually open on their phones. You get a written leak list — not a slide deck.

  2. 02

    First hire

    One named role, one charter you sign, connected to your Jobber / Housecall Pro / ServiceTitan (or the honest recommendation if you do not have one yet). Three live tests before it stays on.

  3. 03

    Crew expansion

    When the first desk is boring, we add the next name — Follow-Up Closer, Review Desk, Membership Clerk — each with its own charter. You own the team. We can step back.

Digital employees

A roster you can read. Not a folder of Zaps.

Each name has a shift, a workplace, a may / may-not list, and a fire switch. Adding work is a staffing decision — update the charter or hire the next seat.

After-Hours Desk

Nights, weekends, overflow. Books open slots. Texts on-call for the emergency list you wrote. Does not quote prices.

Follow-Up Closer

Estimates sitting 48 hours. No-shows. Two pings max. Money objections go to you — the desk does not discount.

Review Desk

Asks once after a clean completed job. Skips fights. Never publishes to Google. Drafts the 1-star reply; you send it.

Membership Clerk

Due visits and renewals on the book you already sold. Books member windows. Does not invent benefits or mix in a review ask.

What we will not do

We do not replace Jobber. We do not sell you a chatbot and leave.

  • We do not rip out the field software your techs already live in.
  • We do not hide the work on our laptop. The workplace is your account.
  • We do not hire one mega-bot that “does everything.” One job per name.
  • We do not need a certification circus to start. We do need a process that will still run if you fire us.
